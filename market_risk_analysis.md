## Why Sovereign-Mind Could Fail (2026 Risks)

### 1. 🎯 Product-Market Fit Risk (Updated)
**The "Simple RAG" Trap**
*   **2026 Reality:** Basic RAG is now a feature in every SaaS tool (Salesforce, Notion, etc.).
*   **Risk:** Building a standalone RAG tool is building a feature, not a product.
*   **Mitigation:** Pivot to **"AI Security Posture Management (AI-SPM)"** for SMEs. Help them manage *all* their local agents and compliance.

### 2. 🛡️ Regulatory & Compliance Risk
**The "SME Compliance" Cliff**
*   **2026 Reality:** EU AI Act is fully live. US state laws (CA, CO, IL) mandate AI transparency and bias audits.
*   **Risk:** SMEs are unprepared and facing fines.
*   **Mitigation:** Add "Compliance Reports" feature. Auto-generate audit logs for EU AI Act / SOC 2.

### 3. 🤖 Security Risk: "Agentic Chaos"
**New 2026 Threat Vector**
*   **Risk:** "Unsafe Output Execution". Valid agents executing malicious commands (e.g., `rm -rf`).
*   **Mitigation:** Implement **"Human-in-the-Loop" (HITL)** controls for all write actions. Sandboxed execution environments.
